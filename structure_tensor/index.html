The structure tensor, the baseline¶

The structure tensor of a volume is the window average of the outer product of its gradient:

\[S = G_\rho * \left(\nabla I_\sigma\, \nabla I_\sigma^{\mathsf T}\right)\]

with the gradient at scale σ and the average over a window ρ. The fibre direction is the eigenvector of the smallest eigenvalue — the direction along which the intensity changes least — and the orientation gap (λ₂ − λ₃) / λ₂ is how unique that direction is. shapetensor.st computes it on the GPU in the layout of the structure-tensor package, so its outputs substitute wherever that package's are consumed.

from shapetensor import st
S = st.structure_tensor_3d(image, 1.0, 10.0)        # (6, Z, Y, X) = [Sxx, Syy, Szz, Sxy, Sxz, Syz]
val, vec = st.eig_special_3d(S)                     # eigenvalues (descending); the smallest's eigenvector, (x, y, z)
vec = st.structure_tensor(image, rho=10.0)          # the same in one call, excised (below)

Where it fails¶

At a tissue interface — the grey/white boundary, the edge of a tract, a vessel wall — the gradients of the step overwhelm those of the fibre texture inside the window, and the direction is dragged into the plane of the boundary: streamlines wrap along it. The size of the effect is the ratio of the step to the texture; at the real tissue's texture level, next to a step of 40 grey levels, it is tens of degrees within 0.1 mm of the boundary.

Where bundles cross, a single tensor reports the bisector, whatever the window.

The excised structure tensor¶

The interface failure has a direct remedy inside the tensor: leave the boundary's gradients out. Gradients whose coarse magnitude (σ = 3 voxels) exceeds three times the median are given zero weight and the window average is renormalised by the surviving weight. Nothing changes in clear tissue (the test suite checks that a block with no step is untouched), and at a step the error drops to the clear-tissue level. The excised structure tensor is the package's baseline, its default structure_tensor, the teacher of the pipeline's stage 2, and the excision is built into every population tensor of stage 3.

Sx = st.excised_structure_tensor_3d(image, 1.0, 10.0, factor=3.0, mask=tissue)

mask is the tissue over which the reference median is taken (without it, the whole block; too low a reference where much of the block is smooth grey matter or background).

A whole volume¶

shapetensor structure-tensor brain.ome.zarr st.zarr --level 0 --rho 10 --chunk 256 256 256

writes S, val and vec chunk by chunk with halos; every chunk equals a one-pass computation (for the excised tensor the reference median is per chunk).

Anisotropic voxels¶

spacing=(dz, dy, dx) declares the voxel size per axis (for example (2.5, 1, 1) for 50 × 20 × 20 µm). The filters are scaled per axis and the gradients divided by the spacing, so the tensor and its directions are in physical coordinates. This is correct only while the coarse axis still resolves the fibre texture; where it does not (fibres running perpendicular to a 50 µm axis at 20 µm fibre spacing), no filtering recovers what the sampling destroyed, and isotropic sampling at the texture scale is the remedy.
