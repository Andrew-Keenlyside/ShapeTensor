1. Shape features¶

The block goes through anatomix, a contrast-agnostic 3-D UNet pretrained for registration across modalities, and the pipeline reads its encoder block 2: 32 channels at half resolution (upsampled to the grid), a receptive field of 16 voxels (0.32 mm at 20 µm). This is the layer where orientation is encoded as a linear code: the mean feature pattern of an oriented voxel is a linear image of its orientation dyad d dᵀ to 98% on phantoms and 79% on a real block (notes/results_log.md §26). Deeper layers (the decoder's layer 58, the output) lose the per-fascicle division: their receptive fields average crossing populations into the bisector before anything can be read.

from shapetensor.features import make_engine
F = make_engine("anatomix_enc2")(block)        # (32, Z, Y, X)

The engine runs the network on tiles of 256³ with 64 voxels of real context on every face and keeps only each tile's core, with reflect padding of the block (the network's own zero padding would put a step at the faces). Its output is unreliable within 24 voxels of a block face; the pipeline's calibration never samples there.

Two other engines exist for comparison: jet, a multiscale Gaussian derivative jet without any network (the control arm), and the deeper anatomix layers anatomix48 and anatomix16.

What the features are, and what they are not¶

A channel of encoder block 2 is a rectified (ReLU) filter response. It is zero over much of the texture and responds where its filter lands on a fibre edge in phase, so a per-voxel read of these features oscillates with the fibre texture (on a fibre / between fibres) the way the raw gradient energy does; the structure tensor is smooth because it squares the gradient and integrates over ρ. The pipeline therefore averages the features over 3³ before any read-out (smooth), and every tensor it builds integrates over ρ as the baseline does. No root or log of the features or of the activations can replace that integration: a pointwise transform moves no power between spatial frequencies (notes/results_log.md §25).

The orientation content of a voxel's features is of second order in the direction — the same content as a structure tensor at the receptive-field scale, with a learned amplitude. That sets what stage 2 can and cannot separate: two populations, yes; a 50/50 mixture at Δθ looks like a weaker single population at the bisector; a third population is linearly dependent on two.
