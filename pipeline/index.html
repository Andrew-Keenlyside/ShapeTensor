The pipeline¶

Four stages, one pair of scales. sigma (the derivative scale) and rho (the integration window) are used alike by the teacher of stage 2, by every population's tensor in stage 3 and by the baseline structure tensor the result is compared with, so every comparison is like for like.

stage what it does module
1. Shape features the block through a pretrained 3-D UNet, read at the encoder layer that encodes orientation shapetensor.features
2. Separating populations the shape space read without labels; the populations present; one activation layer per population shapetensor.separate
3. One tensor per population the image's structure tensor weighted by the population's activation shapetensor.tensors
4. Merging one vector per voxel from the merged tensors, or every population's vector with its activation as weight shapetensor.merge

shapetensor.pipeline runs them: calibrate fits stage 2 on a block, apply runs stages 2–4 with a calibration, population_field does both on one block, run_volume runs a whole zarr volume chunk by chunk.

What is and is not a parameter¶

What each parameter does to the field, varied one at a time on the HiP-CT block: parameters.

  • The feature layer is fixed by evidence (encoder block 2 is where orientation is a linear code; notes/).
  • The teacher is the excised structure tensor at the output rho; its confident sites are the top 30% by orientation gap, stratified over orientation; the least oriented 30% give the background signature.
  • The feature average before any read-out is a 3³ box: the fibre texture's period at 20 µm is about 5 voxels, and the activation of a rectified filter oscillates with that phase; the average integrates it (smooth).
  • The null tau is the activation a wrong population's signature reaches on single-population voxels; it is calibrated on the block and exported with the field, never applied to the vectors.
  • A population is a mode of the window's orientation density that owns at least 5% of the tissue above the null; up to nmax (3).
